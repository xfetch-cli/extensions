<h2 id="before-opening-an-issue" align="center">Before Opening an Issue</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Confirm your platform (Linux/macOS/Windows) and the extension version.</li>
  <li>Verify the extension is installed: <code>xfetch extension list</code>.</li>
  <li>Include the <code>config_providers</code> block, the config being loaded, and reproduction steps.</li>
  <li>Check the <a href="./docs/compatibility.md">compatibility table</a> before reporting platform issues.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="response-expectations" align="center">Response Expectations</h2>

<p>
  Maintainers triage reports based on impact and reproducibility. Clear, minimal reproductions are resolved faster.
</p>
